Privacy Policy

What We Collect
When you create an account, we collect your email address and optional name. We also store the diary entries, moods, Vault entries, test results, and account settings you choose to save so they can follow you across devices.
How Your Data Is Stored
Signed-in account data is stored in Netlify's managed database and cached in your browser for app performance. Existing browser data is moved into your account when you first sign in. You can download or clear your saved data from Settings.
Conversation Analyzer & AI
When you use the Conversation Analyzer, your conversation text or screenshot is sent to Anthropic's API for analysis. We strongly recommend removing names or identifying details before submitting. We do not store your conversation content.
Payments
Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We store Stripe customer and subscription identifiers with your account to confirm access, but we do not store your payment card information.
We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your personal data in any form.
